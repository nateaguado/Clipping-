# GTA 6 launch-week plan

Facts as of 17 Nov 2026. Re-check Rockstar Newswire (rockstargames.com/newswire) before telling the user any date.

## The facts

- **GTA 6 launches Thursday 19 November 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S** (Rockstar Newswire; delayed twice before).
- **No PC version announced.** One unconfirmed report says early 2027. Launch streams are console capture.
- **Single-player at launch.** Twitch's CEO said multiplayer comes "next year" (2027); Rockstar hasn't confirmed it.
  No GTA 6 roleplay servers exist yet; GTA V RP (FiveM, mostly on Kick) keeps running. Don't promise GTA 6 RP clips.
- The Extended Look reveal (Aug 2026) peaked near 3.97 million people watching streams at once: 44% Twitch, 35%
  YouTube, 20% Kick. Expect the launch to be bigger.
- Rockstar's video policy covers non-commercial fan use only. For the reveal it asked creators not to repost the video
  in full without commentary; Twitch made xQc take one reaction down while Kai Cenat and others kept theirs.
- Rockstar's policy on cutscenes: showing them "as part of a larger play-through" is OK, but isolated videos of the
  game's cinematics get removed (quoted by StoryCut, Nov 2026). Leaked or pre-release footage: never.
- Reported (ClipSpeed, citing a YouTube announcement of 12 Nov 2026): a YouTube launch pre-show on 18 Nov at 11 PM ET
  and a 24-hour launch stream from 19 Nov 12 AM ET with creators including Caylus, Jelly and Jake Baldino. Check before
  relying on it.
- Clipping campaigns (mid-Nov 2026, from ClipSpeed and StoryCut, check Content Rewards for today's list): a GTA 6
  sound campaign ($0.70 per 1,000 views, TikTok and Instagram, the campaign's song required), GTA RP streamer
  campaigns around $1.50 to $2 per 1,000 views (some need an application with a sample clip). Rockstar and Take-Two
  run no clipping campaigns. More launch campaigns are expected around 19 Nov.
- YouTube (Sept 2026): graphic game violence in the first 7 seconds makes a video ineligible for ads; pure NPC-kill
  compilations can be demonetised.

## Who to watch

Kai Cenat (Twitch; promised a 100% completion marathon of GTA 6, already played every earlier GTA on stream in Nov
2026), IShowSpeed (YouTube; his own Extended Look reaction has 4.4M views), xQc (Kick/Twitch), N3on and Adin Ross
(Kick; N3on runs the biggest known paid clipper program), Jynxzi, Agent00, CaseOh, Fanum, Hasan, Asmongold.
GTA V RP on Kick is dominated by Arabic-speaking streamers (3mr, sxb, drb7h): a gap for Arabic-speaking clippers.

Check each for a clipping campaign (Content Rewards on Whop, `WHOP.md`) or a program before clipping for money.

## What to clip

1. **First reactions:** first minutes in Vice City, the opening mission, the first big set-piece.
2. **"Streamer finds X":** parodies of real people, brands and streamers; easter eggs; a lookalike of themselves.
3. **Rage and fails:** mission fails, police chases gone wrong, physics chaos.
4. **Marathon milestones:** the moment he finally finishes a mission he failed for hours (as the hook, not a label).
5. **Glitches and funny NPCs.**
6. **Comparisons:** "GTA 6 vs GTA 5 driving" reactions.
7. **Verdicts:** "Is GTA 6 worth it?" moments.

Never post faked "GTA 6 gameplay" (AI fakes got millions of views and were exposed). Never post story spoilers as the
title in the first days: hide them behind the hook ("Kai wasn't ready for this mission").

## How to run launch week

- While a big streamer is live: run `live.py watch <streamer>` in the background and finish the moments it saves
  every few minutes. This beats every clipper who waits for the stream to end.
- Every few hours: `find.py hot <streamer> --days 1` for each Twitch or Kick streamer on the list (YouTube streamers
  like IShowSpeed: `find.py vods <channel>` then `scan` the newest stream). The top viewer clips are what's
  blowing up right now: clip them first.
- For a stream still going: `find.py vods <streamer>` shows the live recording; `scan` it with `--from` set to the
  last hour you checked.
- Speed over polish: one still check, then render. Aim to post within 1 to 3 hours of the moment.
- Title and caption: streamer name + "GTA 6" (people search this) + what happens. Tags: #gta6 #gtavi
  #grandtheftauto6 #<streamer> #streamerclips.
- Weekly: a "Streamers react to ..." YouTube compilation of the biggest shared moment, one chapter per streamer.
- After launch: PC release (unconfirmed, maybe 2027) and GTA 6 Online (2027 per Twitch's CEO) will each start a
  second wave. Until then, GTA V RP stays a strong lane.
