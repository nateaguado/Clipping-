# Getting paid: Content Rewards on Whop

Checked 17 Nov 2026. **Content Rewards** is the biggest clipping marketplace. Streamers and brands put up money and a
rate per 1,000 views; clippers post clips, submit the links, and get paid for the views. It started on Whop and has
two front doors that lead to the same campaigns: **whop.com/contentrewards** (click **Join**, it's free) and
**contentrewards.com** (click **Create Account**). It's run by Content Rewards Inc., and **Whop pays the money out**.
Websites change: if what they see doesn't match a step, ask for a screenshot and guide them from that. Never guess.

How to guide them: one step per message, wait for "done", exact button names in **bold**, and end each step with
"If anything looks different, send me a screenshot and I'll help." They make the account, connect their pages, apply
and submit themselves. Never log in, sign up, apply or submit for them.

Before they start, set expectations in plain words (`PLAYBOOK.md` part 1):
- Most campaigns pay about **$1 per 1,000 views** (rates seen run from about $0.20 to $6). 100,000 views is about $100.
- A clip earns nothing until it passes the campaign's **minimum** (often $2, so about 2,000 views) and stops at its
  **cap** (often $35 to $300 per clip).
- Content Rewards keeps **10%**. Money arrives about **10 days after a clip is approved**.
- Views have to be checked; plan on 20 to 30% never counting. Fake or bought views get accounts banned and the
  money held.
- Never pay anyone to join a campaign. Joining Content Rewards is free.

## Step 1: Join Content Rewards on Whop

> 1. Open your web browser and go to **whop.com/contentrewards**.
> 2. Click the blue **Join** button (on the right, next to "Content Rewards"). It's free.
> 3. If you don't have a Whop account yet, it asks you to make one: **Continue with Google** is easiest, or use your
>    email and type the code they send you.
> 4. Tell me "I'm in" when you can see the Content Rewards page with a menu on the left (**Start Here**, **Link Social
>    Accounts**, **Discover Campaigns**...).

(The same campaigns are also at **contentrewards.com**, button **Create Account**. Campaign pages open there, and
you may be asked to log in once with the same Google account or email. That's normal.)

## Step 2: Link the pages they'll post from

Only clips posted from pages linked to Content Rewards can be submitted.

> 1. In the menu on the left, click **Link Social Accounts**.
> 2. Follow what it says to link your **TikTok** clip page, then your **YouTube** and **Instagram** clip pages. It may
>    ask you to log in to that app, or to put a short code in your bio for a minute to prove the page is yours. That's
>    normal.
> 3. Tell me "linked" when your pages show as connected.

## Step 3: Find a GTA campaign

> 1. In the menu on the left, click **Discover Campaigns** (on contentrewards.com it's **Discover** at the top).
> 2. Type **GTA** in the search box (also try **GTA 6**, **GTA RP** and the names of streamers you like).
> 3. Click a campaign to open it. Send me a screenshot of its page and I'll explain it in plain words.

When they send it, read them: who or what you may clip, the rate per 1,000 views, the minimum and cap per clip, the
budget left (skip campaigns with under about 10% left: late views may never get paid), which apps are allowed, and
every rule (credits or handles on screen, hashtags, length, a required song, no other tool's watermark). Save the
rules in `~/Stream Clips/channels/<campaign>.md`.

What to look for:
- **Streamer campaigns** name the streamers you may clip. Only clip a streamer a campaign names (or one who allows
  fan clips, for your own page).
- **Game campaigns** pay for GTA gameplay or reaction clips.
- **Sound campaigns** pay for clips that use a certain song: post those from the app with the song added.
- Some need an **application**: a sample clip and a few words. Make them a great sample clip first, then they apply.
- As of mid-November 2026 there were only a few GTA campaigns (GTA RP streamers, one sound campaign). More are expected
  around the 19 November launch: check Discover every day of launch week.

## Step 4: Join it

> 1. On the campaign page, click **Join** (or **Apply**).
> 2. Read the rules and tick that you've read them.
> 3. Tell me "joined" (or "applied": approval can take up to 2 days).

## Step 5: Submit a clip (every clip, right after it's posted)

> 1. Post the clip from one of your connected pages (or I post it for you with Blotato).
> 2. Open the post on your phone or computer and copy its link (on the app: **Share**, then **Copy link**).
> 3. Open the campaign's page (from **Discover Campaigns**, or the campaign link) and click **Submit clip** (or
>    **Submit**).
> 4. Paste the link: hold **Command ⌘** and tap **V** (Windows: **Ctrl** + **V**). Tick that it follows the rules,
>    then click **Submit**.
> 5. Tell me "submitted".

**Timing:** Content Rewards' own guide says submit **within 30 minutes of posting**; some campaigns set their own
window. Views before the submission may not count.

## Step 6: Track and get paid

> - Your submitted clips show under **My submissions** (or **Earnings**): pending, approved or rejected.
> - Approved views are paid up to the campaign's cap, until its budget runs out.
> - To take the money out, Whop asks for a one-time ID check and your bank or PayPal details. You do this yourself
>   on Whop's page; I never see it.

## Why clips get rejected (and the fix)

- A required credit, handle or hashtag is missing: put it in the caption or on screen (`credit` in clip.json).
- Wrong source: a streamer or game the campaign didn't name.
- Posted from a page that isn't connected in Content Rewards.
- A plain repost with no edit, another clipping tool's watermark, or a near-copy of another clip.
- Submitted late, or after the budget ran out.
- Views that look bought or botted.
Content Rewards gives **one appeal** per rejected clip: help them write a short, polite reason ("The clip follows rule
3: the streamer's handle is shown at 0:01").
